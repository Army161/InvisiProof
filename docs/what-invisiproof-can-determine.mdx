# What InvisiProof Can and Cannot Determine

## What it can assess

InvisiProof analyzes observable signals in submitted content:

- **Images**: visual inconsistencies, metadata patterns, compression artifacts, text within images, layout anomalies
- **Text/Messages**: linguistic patterns, urgency indicators, request patterns, structural signals
- **URLs**: domain age, structure, known pattern indicators (without visiting the URL from your device)

## What it cannot determine

InvisiProof **cannot**:

- Guarantee a person's identity
- Confirm a transaction is legitimate or fraudulent
- Provide legal advice or evidence
- Replace professional investigation
- Detect all forms of deception

## How to interpret results

Results are **risk assessments**, not verdicts. A "Low Risk" result means the observable signals do not indicate high risk—it does not mean the content is safe. A "High Risk" result means observable signals suggest elevated risk—it does not mean the content is definitely harmful.

Always use results as one input among many. Consult appropriate professionals for high-stakes decisions.

InvisiProof results are not legal evidence, professional opinions, or guarantees of safety or harm. See the [AI Disclaimer](/legal/ai-disclaimer) for full details.
