# Quickstart

## 1. Create your account

Open InvisiProof and tap **Sign Up**. Enter your email and a password, then verify your email address.

You can also sign in with Google or Apple.

## 2. Run your first assessment

Tap the **Scan** tab at the bottom of the screen.

Choose one of three options:
- **Analyze Image** — select a screenshot from your photo library or take a photo
- **Analyze Text** — paste a suspicious message or email
- **Analyze URL** — enter a link you want to check

Review the consent notice and tap **Upload Securely** (for images) or **Save Securely** (for text/URLs).

## 3. Request analysis

After uploading, tap **Request Analysis Now** to start the risk assessment.

Results include:
- Risk level (Low, Medium, High, or Critical)
- Risk score (0–100)
- Summary of observable signals
- Warning indicators
- Recommended next steps
- Limitations of the assessment

## 4. Create a Proof Request (optional)

Tap the **Requests** tab, then **Create Request**.

Fill in the purpose and instructions, then share the generated code or link with the other person.

They submit evidence privately. You receive only the risk assessment result.
