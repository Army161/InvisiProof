# Quickstart

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Open InvisiProof and tap **Sign Up**. Enter your email and a password, then verify your email address by clicking the link sent to your inbox.

    You can also sign in with **Google** for a faster setup.

    <Note>
    Apple Sign-In is not available on Android. Use email/password or Google to create your account.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run your first assessment">
    Tap the **Scan** tab at the bottom of the screen.

    Choose one of three options:

    - **Analyze Image** — select a screenshot from your photo library or take a photo
    - **Analyze Text** — paste a suspicious message or email
    - **Analyze URL** — enter a link you want to check

    Review the consent notice and tap **Upload Securely** (for images) or **Save Securely** (for text/URLs).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Request analysis">
    After uploading, tap **Request Analysis Now** to start the risk assessment.

    Results include:
    - **Risk Level**: Low, Medium, High, or Critical
    - **Risk Score**: 0–100
    - **Summary**: Plain-language description of what was found
    - **Warning Indicators**: Specific signals identified
    - **Recommended Actions**: Suggested next steps
    - **Limitations**: What this assessment cannot determine
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a Proof Request (optional)">
    Tap the **Requests** tab, then **Create Request**.

    Fill in the purpose and instructions, then share the generated 6-digit code with the other person.

    They submit evidence privately. You receive only the risk assessment result — never the raw evidence.
  </Step>
</Steps>
