# InvisiProof

**Request proof. Verify the risk. Protect the evidence.**

InvisiProof is a privacy-first trust and risk-verification platform. It helps you evaluate suspicious images, messages, and URLs—and lets another person submit evidence privately so you can receive a risk assessment without automatically receiving the raw evidence.

## What you can do

- **Analyze images and screenshots** — Upload a suspicious screenshot or photo for risk signal analysis
- **Analyze text and messages** — Paste a suspicious message, email, or conversation for assessment
- **Analyze URLs** — Submit a link for risk analysis without the app visiting it from your device
- **Create a Proof Request** — Ask someone to submit evidence privately; receive only the normalized risk result

## What InvisiProof is not

InvisiProof does not guarantee that a person, message, document, URL, or transaction is authentic, legitimate, safe, fraudulent, or legally valid. It provides risk assessments based on observable signals in the submitted content.

Results are labeled as **risk assessments** with clear limitations, warning indicators, and recommended next steps.

## Privacy first

- Raw evidence submitted through Proof Requests is stored privately and scoped so requesters cannot read it
- Analytics never collect submitted images, text, URLs, or message content
- You can delete your data at any time
