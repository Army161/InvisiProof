# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: January 2025**

InvisiProof ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account information
When you create an account, we collect your email address and, if you sign up with Google or Apple, the information provided by those services (name, email).

### Submitted content
When you use InvisiProof to analyze images, text, or URLs, that content is stored privately in your account. When you respond to a Proof Request, your submission is stored with scoped access controls.

### Usage data
We collect analytics events about how you use the app — screen views, feature usage, errors. We do not include submitted content in analytics events.

### Device information
We collect your device type, operating system version, and app version for diagnostic purposes.

### Payment information
Subscription payments are processed by Apple (iOS) or Google (Android). We do not receive or store your payment card details.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To provide and operate InvisiProof
- To process risk assessments
- To manage your account and subscription
- To send transactional emails (account verification, password reset)
- To diagnose and fix technical issues
- To improve the product based on usage patterns

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We share information with:

- **AI providers**: submitted content is processed by AI models to produce assessments. The default provider is InvisiProof's server infrastructure. If you configure BYOK, your content is sent to your chosen provider.
- **Cloud infrastructure providers**: for storage and hosting
- **Authentication providers**: for account management (Google, Apple)
- **Law enforcement or legal process**: when required by law or to protect rights and safety

## 4. Data Retention

Assessment history is retained according to your plan (7 days for Free, 90 days for Plus, unlimited for Pro and Max). Submitted content is retained until you delete it or your account is deleted. Account information is retained while your account is active.

## 5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access the personal information we hold about you
- Delete your account and associated data
- Correct inaccurate information
- Object to certain processing

To exercise these rights, contact privacy@invisiproof.com or use the in-app deletion tools.

## 6. Children

InvisiProof is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## 7. Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest for stored content. We implement access controls to limit who can access your data.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify you of material changes through the app or by email. Continued use of InvisiProof after changes constitutes acceptance.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests: privacy@invisiproof.com
