# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** January 1, 2025

**Last updated:** January 1, 2025

InvisiProof ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the InvisiProof mobile application and web application (collectively, the "Service").

Please read this policy carefully. By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account information

When you create an account, we collect:
- Email address
- Password (stored as a bcrypt hash — we never store your plain-text password)
- Authentication tokens (for Google Sign-In)

### 1.2 Content you submit

When you use the Service, you may submit:
- **Images** — screenshots, photos, or documents uploaded for analysis
- **Text** — messages, emails, or conversations pasted for analysis
- **URLs** — links submitted for structural analysis

This content is stored privately in your account with row-level security. It is used solely to produce your risk assessment.

### 1.3 Assessment results

We store the results of your risk assessments, including risk level, risk score, warning indicators, and recommended actions.

### 1.4 BYOK API keys

If you configure a Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) provider, your API key is stored encrypted in our database. It is never stored in plain text and is never logged.

### 1.5 Usage analytics

We collect anonymized usage events through PostHog, including:
- App opens, feature usage, and assessment outcomes (risk level, scan type)
- No content (images, text, URLs) is included in analytics events
- No personally identifiable information is sent to PostHog

See [Analytics](/privacy/analytics) for the full list of tracked events.

### 1.6 Technical information

We may collect:
- Device type and operating system version (for troubleshooting)
- App version
- Error logs (without content)

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide the Service (run risk assessments, store results, manage your account)
- Process AI analysis of submitted content
- Manage your subscription through RevenueCat
- Send transactional emails (account verification, password reset)
- Improve the Service through anonymized analytics
- Respond to support requests
- Enforce our Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy
- Comply with legal obligations

We do **not**:
- Sell your personal information to third parties
- Use your submitted content to train AI models
- Share your content with other users
- Use your content for advertising

## 3. Third-Party Services

InvisiProof uses the following third-party services:

| Service | Purpose | Data shared |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| **Supabase** | Database, storage, authentication, edge functions | Account data, content, results |
| **PostHog** | Product analytics | Anonymized usage events (no content) |
| **RevenueCat** | Subscription management | Subscription status, purchase history |
| **OpenAI / Anthropic / Google Gemini** | AI analysis | Content submitted for analysis (when using InvisiProof's infrastructure or your BYOK key) |
| **Google** | Google Sign-In | Email address, Google account ID |

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them.

For BYOK users: your content is sent to your chosen AI provider under your own API account. InvisiProof does not control how your provider handles that data.

---

## 4. Data Retention

We retain your data as follows:
- **Free plan**: Scans and results retained for 7 days
- **Plus plan**: Scans and results retained for 90 days
- **Pro/Max plans**: Scans and results retained until you delete them
- **Account data**: Retained until you delete your account
- **Analytics**: Retained by PostHog for up to 1 year (anonymized)

See [Data Retention](/privacy/data-retention) for full details.

---

## 5. Data Security

We protect your data using:
- TLS 1.2+ encryption for all data in transit
- AES-256 encryption for sensitive fields at rest (including BYOK keys)
- Row-level security (RLS) policies enforced at the PostgreSQL level
- Private storage buckets with no public URLs
- Signed URLs with short expiry for image access

We follow industry-standard security practices. However, no system is perfectly secure. If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it to security@invisiproof.com.

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

- **Access**: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Correction**: Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your data (see [Delete Account](/account/delete-account))
- **Portability**: Request your data in a portable format
- **Objection**: Object to certain processing of your data
- **Opt-out of analytics**: Disable analytics in Profile → Privacy Settings

To exercise these rights, contact support@invisiproof.com with the subject line "Data Subject Request". We will respond within 30 days.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

InvisiProof is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact support@invisiproof.com and we will delete it.

InvisiProof is operated from the United States. If you are located outside the United States, your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate. By using the Service, you consent to this transfer.
