# Changelog

## v1.0.0-alpha

**Initial release**

### Features

- **Direct assessments**: Analyze images, text, and URLs for risk signals
- **Proof Requests**: Two-party private evidence workflow — requesters receive risk assessments, not raw evidence
- **Risk levels**: Low, Medium, High, and Critical with 0–100 risk scores
- **Warning indicators**: Specific observable signals identified in each assessment
- **Trust levels**: device_generated and server_verified assessment provenance
- **BYOK**: Bring Your Own Key support for Pro and Max plans
- **Assessment history**: View, manage, and delete past assessments
- **Four plans**: Free, Plus ($14.99/mo), Pro ($34.99/mo), Max ($79.99/mo)
- **Annual billing**: Save up to $359.89/year on annual plans
- **Privacy controls**: Delete individual submissions or your entire account at any time
- **Sign in options**: Email/password, Google, Apple

### Known limitations

- This is an alpha release. Features and pricing may change.
- Assessment accuracy depends on the quality and completeness of submitted content.
- Results are risk assessments, not verdicts. See the [AI Disclaimer](/legal/ai-disclaimer).

### Coming soon

- Android support
- Additional AI provider options
- Enhanced Proof Request workflows
