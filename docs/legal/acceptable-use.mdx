# Acceptable Use Policy

**Last updated: January 2025**

This Acceptable Use Policy describes what you may and may not do with InvisiProof.

## Permitted uses

- Analyzing suspicious content you have received or encountered
- Creating Proof Requests to evaluate claims made to you
- Responding to Proof Requests you have received
- Using assessment results to inform your own decisions

## Prohibited uses

### Illegal activity
Do not use InvisiProof to facilitate illegal activity, including fraud, harassment, stalking, or unauthorized surveillance.

### Submitting others' private content without authorization
Do not submit images, messages, or other content belonging to others without their knowledge and consent, except where you have a legitimate reason (such as evaluating content sent to you).

### Harassment and targeting
Do not use InvisiProof to build a case against, harass, or target a specific individual. The Service is designed for evaluating content you have received, not for investigating people.

### Automated or bulk submission
Do not use automated tools, scripts, or bots to submit content at scale. InvisiProof is designed for individual use.

### Circumventing limits
Do not attempt to circumvent usage limits, access controls, or subscription restrictions.

### Misrepresenting results
Do not present InvisiProof assessment results as legal evidence, professional opinions, or definitive proof of fraud or harm. Results are risk assessments with clear limitations.

### Submitting illegal content
Do not submit content that is illegal to possess or distribute, including child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or content that violates applicable law.

## Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in account suspension or termination. We reserve the right to investigate suspected violations and to cooperate with law enforcement where required.

## Reporting abuse

If you believe someone is using InvisiProof in violation of this policy, contact support@invisiproof.com.
