# Acceptable Use Policy

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") describes what you may and may not do when using InvisiProof. Violations may result in account suspension or termination.

---

## Prohibited content

You may not submit the following content to InvisiProof for analysis:

- **Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)** — any content that sexually exploits or depicts minors. This is illegal and will be reported to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant law enforcement
- **Content that facilitates violence** — content that plans, promotes, or facilitates violence against specific individuals or groups
- **Illegal content** — content that is illegal in your jurisdiction or the jurisdiction where InvisiProof operates
- **Content you do not have the right to submit** — content owned by others that you do not have permission to share or process

---

## Prohibited uses

You may not use InvisiProof to:

### Security and integrity violations
- Attempt to gain unauthorized access to InvisiProof's systems, databases, or other users' accounts
- Circumvent, disable, or interfere with security features of the Service
- Probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Service without authorization (see [Security Reporting](/privacy/security-reporting) for authorized security research)
- Introduce malware, viruses, or other malicious code

### Reverse engineering and competitive use
- Reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or attempt to derive the source code of the Service
- Use the Service to build a competing product or service
- Systematically scrape, crawl, or extract data from the Service using automated means
- Access the Service through unauthorized APIs or scripts

### Abuse and harassment
- Harass, threaten, intimidate, or harm other users or InvisiProof staff
- Use the Service to stalk or monitor individuals without their knowledge or consent
- Submit other people's private information without their consent

### Fraud and misrepresentation
- Impersonate InvisiProof, its employees, or other users
- Submit false or misleading information to manipulate assessment results
- Use assessment results in a misleading way (e.g. presenting a Low Risk result as a guarantee of safety)
- Use the Service to facilitate fraud against third parties

### Legal violations
- Use the Service in violation of any applicable law or regulation
- Use the Service to violate others' intellectual property rights
- Use the Service in a way that violates privacy laws (e.g. submitting others' private data without consent)

---

## Enforcement

InvisiProof reserves the right to:
- Remove content that violates this AUP
- Suspend or terminate accounts that violate this AUP
- Report illegal content or activity to law enforcement
- Cooperate with law enforcement investigations

We may act without notice for serious violations (e.g. CSAM, active security attacks). For less severe violations, we may issue a warning before taking action.

---

## Appeals

If you believe your account was suspended or terminated in error, contact support@invisiproof.com with the subject line "Account Appeal". Include:
- Your account email address
- A description of what happened
- Why you believe the action was taken in error

We will review appeals within 5 business days.

---

## Reporting violations

If you believe another user is violating this AUP, contact support@invisiproof.com with:
- A description of the violation
- Any evidence you have (screenshots, etc.)

For security vulnerabilities, see [Security Reporting](/privacy/security-reporting).
