# AI and Risk Assessment Disclaimer

**Read this before relying on any InvisiProof result.**

## Results are risk assessments, not verdicts

InvisiProof produces **risk assessments** based on observable signals in submitted content. A risk assessment is not:

- A determination that content is fraudulent, harmful, or illegal
- A determination that content is safe, legitimate, or authentic
- Legal advice or legal evidence
- A professional opinion (medical, financial, legal, or otherwise)
- A guarantee of any kind

## AI limitations

InvisiProof uses AI models to analyze submitted content. AI models:

- Can produce incorrect results
- Can miss signals that a human expert would catch
- Can flag signals that are not actually indicative of risk
- Are not infallible and are not a substitute for professional judgment

## What "Low Risk" means

A Low risk result means the observable signals in the submitted content do not indicate elevated risk. It does **not** mean:

- The content is safe
- The person or entity behind the content is trustworthy
- The transaction or claim is legitimate

## What "High Risk" or "Critical" means

A High or Critical risk result means the observable signals suggest elevated risk. It does **not** mean:

- The content is definitely fraudulent or harmful
- The person or entity behind the content has done anything wrong
- You should take any specific action
