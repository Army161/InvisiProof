# Subscription Refunds

InvisiProof subscriptions on Android are processed through Google Play. This page explains how refunds work and how to request one.

---

## Google Play refund policy

Because InvisiProof subscriptions are purchased through Google Play, refunds are primarily handled by Google according to their refund policy:

- **Within 48 hours of purchase**: You may be eligible for a full refund through Google Play
- **After 48 hours**: Refunds are at Google's discretion

Google Play's refund policy applies to all InvisiProof purchases made through the Android app.

### How to request a refund through Google Play

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Google Play">
    Open the Google Play Store app on your Android device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to order history">
    Tap your profile icon → **Payments and subscriptions** → **Budget and history** → **Order history**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the InvisiProof purchase">
    Locate the InvisiProof subscription charge you want to refund.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request a refund">
    Tap the order → **Request a refund** and follow Google's prompts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Alternatively, visit [play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions) on the web.

## InvisiProof's refund policy

### No refunds for used assessments

InvisiProof does not issue refunds for assessments that have already been used. If you have run assessments during a billing period, that period is considered used and is not eligible for a refund from InvisiProof directly.

### Billing errors

If you were charged incorrectly — for example, charged twice for the same period, charged for a plan you didn't select, or charged after cancellation — contact support@invisiproof.com with:
- Your account email address
- The date and amount of the incorrect charge
- Your Google Play order number (if available)

We will investigate and work with Google Play to resolve billing errors.

### Subscription cancellation

Cancelling your subscription stops future charges. It does not generate a refund for the current billing period. You retain access to paid features until the end of the current period.

### Plan downgrades

Downgrading to a lower plan does not generate a refund for the difference. The downgrade takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

---

## Exceptions

We may consider refunds in exceptional circumstances, including:
- Technical failures that prevented you from using the Service for a significant portion of a billing period
- Duplicate charges due to a technical error
- Charges after a confirmed cancellation

To request an exception, contact support@invisiproof.com with documentation of the issue.
