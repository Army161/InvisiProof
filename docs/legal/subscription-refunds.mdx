# Subscriptions and Refunds

## How subscriptions work

InvisiProof subscriptions are purchased and managed through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). InvisiProof does not process payments directly.

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period unless cancelled.

## Billing

- **Monthly plans**: billed once per month on the same day as your subscription start date
- **Annual plans**: billed once per year on the same day as your subscription start date

## Cancellation

To cancel your subscription:

**iOS**: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → InvisiProof → Cancel Subscription

**Android**: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → InvisiProof → Cancel

After cancellation, your plan remains active until the end of the current billing period. After that, your account reverts to the Free plan. You do not lose access immediately upon cancellation.

## Refunds

InvisiProof cannot issue refunds directly. Refund requests must be submitted to Apple or Google:

**iOS refunds**: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)

**Android refunds**: Contact Google Play support

Refund eligibility is determined by Apple's and Google's refund policies, not by InvisiProof.

## Downgrades

If you downgrade to a lower plan, the change takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You keep your current plan's features until then.

## Free plan

The Free plan is available to all users at no cost. It includes 2 assessments per month, 1 Proof Request per month, and 7-day history.

## Questions

For subscription questions, contact support@invisiproof.com. Note that we cannot process refunds — those must go through Apple or Google.
