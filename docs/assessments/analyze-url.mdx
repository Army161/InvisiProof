# Analyze a URL

Use URL analysis to evaluate a suspicious link for risk signals. InvisiProof analyzes the URL structure and available signals **without visiting the URL from your device**.

## How to analyze a URL

1. Tap the **Scan** tab at the bottom of the screen
2. Tap **Analyze URL**
3. Paste or type the URL you want to analyze
4. Review the consent notice
5. Tap **Save Securely** to store the URL privately in your account
6. Tap **Request Analysis Now** to start the assessment

## Important: InvisiProof does not visit the URL from your device

When you submit a URL, InvisiProof analyzes it based on its structure and available signals. **Your device does not navigate to the URL.** This means:

- You are not exposed to any content the URL might load
- No cookies, tracking pixels, or scripts from the URL are executed on your device
- The URL is not "clicked" on your behalf

<Warning>
Do not manually open a URL you believe is suspicious before or after analysis. The analysis result does not make a URL safe to visit.
</Warning>

## What gets analyzed

InvisiProof looks for observable signals including:

- Domain structure and age indicators
- Subdomain patterns associated with phishing
- URL path patterns (encoded characters, unusual structures)
- Known pattern indicators in the domain or path
- Mismatch between displayed text and actual destination (if provided)

## What you receive

After analysis, you see:

- **Risk Level**: Low, Medium, High, or Critical
- **Risk Score**: 0–100
- **Summary**: Plain-language description of what was found
- **Warning Indicators**: Specific signals identified
- **Recommended Actions**: Suggested next steps
- **Limitations**: What this assessment cannot determine

## Privacy

The URL is stored privately in your account. It is not shared with other users. You can delete it at any time from your scan history.
