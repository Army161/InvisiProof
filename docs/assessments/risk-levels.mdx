# Risk Levels

Every InvisiProof assessment includes a **risk level** that summarizes the observable signals found in the submitted content.

## The four levels

### Low Risk

Observable signals do not indicate elevated risk. The content does not show patterns commonly associated with fraud, phishing, or deception.

**What this means**: The signals reviewed do not raise concerns. This does not guarantee the content is safe or legitimate.

### Medium Risk

Some observable signals suggest caution is warranted. The content shows one or more patterns that appear in higher-risk content, but the overall signal is mixed.

**What this means**: Proceed carefully. Verify claims through independent channels before acting.

### High Risk

Multiple observable signals indicate elevated risk. The content shows patterns strongly associated with fraud, phishing, impersonation, or deception.

**What this means**: Do not act on this content without independent verification. Avoid sending money, credentials, or personal information.

### Critical Risk

Strong observable signals indicate very high risk. The content shows patterns that are highly consistent with known fraud, phishing, or harmful content.

**What this means**: Do not act on this content. Consider reporting it to the appropriate platform, institution, or authority.

## Risk score

Each assessment also includes a **risk score** from 0 to 100. Higher scores indicate more risk signals were found. The score provides more granularity than the four-level label.

| Score Range | Typical Level |
|-------------|---------------|
| 0–24 | Low |
| 25–49 | Medium |
| 50–74 | High |
| 75–100 | Critical |

Exact thresholds may vary based on content type and context.

## Important limitations

Risk levels are based on observable signals only. A Low risk result does not mean content is safe. A Critical risk result does not mean content is definitively harmful. Always use results as one input among many.
