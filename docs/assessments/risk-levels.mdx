# Risk Levels

Every InvisiProof assessment produces a risk level based on the density and severity of observable signals in the submitted content. There are four risk levels.

## Risk level overview

| Risk Level | Score Range | Color | Meaning |
|------------|-------------|-------|---------|
| Low | 0–24 | Green | Few or no risk signals detected |
| Medium | 25–49 | Yellow | Some risk signals present |
| High | 50–74 | Orange | Multiple significant risk signals |
| Critical | 75–100 | Red | Strong concentration of high-severity signals |

<Warning>
Risk levels are based on observable signals only. A Low risk level does not mean content is safe or authentic. A Critical risk level does not mean content is definitely harmful or fraudulent. Always use results as one input among many.
</Warning>

## Low Risk (0–24)

<Card title="Low Risk" icon="circle-check">
  **What it means:** The observable signals analyzed do not indicate elevated risk. The content does not exhibit patterns commonly associated with fraud, phishing, manipulation, or deception.

  **What it does NOT mean:** The content is safe, authentic, or legitimate. Sophisticated deception may produce low risk scores.

  **Recommended actions:**
  - No immediate action required based on observable signals
  - Continue to exercise normal judgment
  - If the content involves a high-stakes decision, seek additional verification
  - Consider using a [Proof Request](/proof-requests/overview) if you need independent evidence
</Card>

## Medium Risk (25–49)

<Card title="Medium Risk" icon="circle-exclamation">
  **What it means:** Some risk signals are present in the observable content. The content exhibits patterns that warrant caution, though they may also appear in legitimate content.

  **Common causes:**
  - Mild urgency language that could be legitimate
  - Unusual formatting or structure
  - A URL with a slightly unusual domain structure
  - Text that contains some but not all hallmarks of a scam

  **Recommended actions:**
  - Proceed with caution
  - Verify the sender or source through an independent channel (call the company directly, not using contact info in the message)
  - Do not click links or provide information until you have verified the source
  - Review the specific warning indicators for guidance on what to check
</Card>

## High Risk (50–74)

<Card title="High Risk" icon="triangle-exclamation">
  **What it means:** Multiple significant risk signals are present. The content exhibits patterns strongly associated with fraud, phishing, impersonation, or manipulation.

  **Common causes:**
  - Urgency language combined with financial requests
  - Clear impersonation of a known brand or institution
  - URL structure matching known phishing patterns
  - Image with visible editing artifacts in a financial document
  - Multiple warning indicators across different categories

  **Recommended actions:**
  - Do not act on the content without independent verification
  - Do not click any links in the content
  - Do not provide any personal, financial, or credential information
  - Contact the purported sender through official channels (look up the number yourself, do not use contact info in the message)
  - Consider reporting to relevant authorities (your bank, the FTC, Action Fraud, etc.)
  - If this involves a financial transaction, contact your bank immediately
</Card>

## Critical Risk (75–100)

<Card title="Critical Risk" icon="circle-xmark">
  **What it means:** A strong concentration of high-severity risk signals is present. The content exhibits multiple hallmarks of fraud, phishing, or manipulation at high severity.

  **Common causes:**
  - Classic phishing email with urgency, impersonation, and a suspicious link
  - Fake invoice or payment request with editing artifacts
  - Romance scam message with financial pressure and emotional manipulation
  - URL matching known phishing kit patterns with typosquatted domain

  **Recommended actions:**
  - Do not act on this content
  - Do not click any links
  - Do not provide any information to the sender
  - Report to relevant authorities:
    - **Financial fraud**: Contact your bank and report to the FTC (reportfraud.ftc.gov) or Action Fraud (UK)
    - **Phishing**: Report to the Anti-Phishing Working Group (reportphishing@apwg.org)
    - **Workplace fraud**: Report to your IT security team
    - **Personal safety**: Contact local law enforcement if you feel threatened
  - If you have already acted on this content (clicked a link, provided credentials, sent money), take immediate protective action
</Card>

## How scores are calculated

The risk score (0–100) is calculated from the weighted severity of all warning indicators detected in the content. Each indicator contributes to the score based on:

- **Category weight** — some signal categories (e.g. financial pressure + impersonation together) carry higher weight
- **Severity** — each indicator is rated low, medium, high, or critical
- **Co-occurrence** — multiple indicators in the same category amplify each other

The score is not a probability of fraud. It is a normalized measure of observable risk signal density.

## Limitations

<Note>
Risk levels reflect observable signals only. They do not account for context you have that the AI does not — your relationship with the sender, the history of your communication, or information you know independently. Always apply your own judgment alongside the assessment result.
</Note>
