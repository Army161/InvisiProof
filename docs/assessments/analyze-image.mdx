# Analyze an Image

Use image analysis to evaluate suspicious screenshots, photos, documents, or any visual content you want to assess for risk signals.

## How to analyze an image

1. Tap the **Scan** tab at the bottom of the screen
2. Tap **Analyze Image**
3. Choose **Photo Library** to select an existing image, or **Camera** to take a new photo
4. Review the consent notice — it explains what will be stored and how it will be used
5. Tap **Upload Securely** to store the image privately in your account
6. Tap **Request Analysis Now** to start the assessment

## What gets analyzed

InvisiProof looks for observable signals including:

- Visual inconsistencies (layout, fonts, spacing anomalies)
- Compression artifacts that may indicate editing
- Text within the image (names, amounts, dates, instructions)
- Structural patterns common in fraudulent documents or screenshots
- Metadata patterns where available

## What you receive

After analysis, you see:

- **Risk Level**: Low, Medium, High, or Critical
- **Risk Score**: 0–100
- **Summary**: Plain-language description of what was found
- **Warning Indicators**: Specific signals identified
- **Recommended Actions**: Suggested next steps
- **Limitations**: What this assessment cannot determine

## Supported image types

InvisiProof accepts common image formats including JPEG, PNG, HEIC, and WebP. Maximum file size depends on your plan.

## Privacy

Your image is stored privately in your account. It is not shared with other users. You can delete it at any time from your scan history.

<Note>
InvisiProof does not visit URLs or links found within images. If you want to analyze a URL, use the [Analyze URL](/assessments/analyze-url) feature separately.
</Note>
