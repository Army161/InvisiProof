# Analyze Text

Use text analysis to evaluate suspicious messages, emails, conversations, or any written content you want to assess for risk signals.

## How to analyze text

1. Tap the **Scan** tab at the bottom of the screen
2. Tap **Analyze Text**
3. Paste or type the text you want to analyze
4. Review the consent notice
5. Tap **Save Securely** to store the text privately in your account
6. Tap **Request Analysis Now** to start the assessment

## What gets analyzed

InvisiProof looks for observable signals including:

- Urgency language and pressure tactics
- Requests for money, credentials, or personal information
- Impersonation patterns (mimicking known brands, institutions, or people)
- Grammatical and structural patterns associated with scam messages
- Inconsistencies between claimed identity and message content
- Links or contact information embedded in the text

## What you receive

After analysis, you see:

- **Risk Level**: Low, Medium, High, or Critical
- **Risk Score**: 0–100
- **Summary**: Plain-language description of what was found
- **Warning Indicators**: Specific signals identified
- **Recommended Actions**: Suggested next steps
- **Limitations**: What this assessment cannot determine

## Tips for better results

- Include the full message, not just a portion
- If the message contains a URL, you can also run a separate [URL analysis](/assessments/analyze-url)
- For email headers or metadata, paste the full visible content

## Privacy

Your text is stored privately in your account. It is not shared with other users. You can delete it at any time from your scan history.
