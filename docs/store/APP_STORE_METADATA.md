## Description (4000 chars max)
InvisiProof helps you evaluate suspicious images, messages, and URLs—and lets another person submit evidence privately so you can receive a risk assessment without automatically receiving the raw evidence.

**Three ways to analyze:**
• Analyze screenshots and images for visual risk signals
• Analyze suspicious text, messages, and emails
• Analyze URLs for risk indicators without visiting them from your device

**Private Proof Requests:**
Ask someone to submit evidence privately. They see a consent notice, submit their evidence, and you receive only the normalized risk assessment—not the raw evidence. This protects both parties.

**Risk assessments include:**
• Risk level (Low, Medium, High, Critical)
• Risk score (0–100)
• Summary of observable signals
• Warning indicators
• Recommended next steps
• Clear limitations

**Privacy first:**
• Evidence stored privately with scoped access
• Analytics never collect submitted content
• Delete your data at any time
• BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) support for advanced users

InvisiProof does not guarantee that any person, message, document, URL, or transaction is authentic, legitimate, safe, or fraudulent. Results are risk assessments based on observable signals.
