# Respondent Guide

This guide covers what to do when someone sends you a Proof Request link or code.

## What is a Proof Request?

A Proof Request is an invitation to submit evidence privately. The person who sent it will receive a risk assessment of what you submit — not automatically the raw evidence itself.

You are in control of what you submit. You can also choose not to respond.

## How to respond

1. Open the link or enter the code at [invisiproof.com](https://invisiproof.com) or in the InvisiProof app
2. Read the **purpose** and **instructions** set by the requester
3. Review the **consent notice** — it explains what will happen with your submission
4. Choose what to submit: an image, text, or URL
5. Tap **Submit Privately**

You do not need an InvisiProof account to respond to a request.

## What happens after you submit

- Your submission is stored privately and assessed for risk signals
- The requester receives only the risk assessment result
- You can delete your submission at any time (see below)

## Deleting your submission

If you want to remove what you submitted:

1. Open the same request link
2. Tap **Delete My Submission**

Deleting your submission removes the raw evidence from storage. The requester's assessment result may remain, but it will no longer be linked to your content.

## What the requester can see

The requester sees the risk assessment result. They do not automatically receive your raw images, text, or files. See [Privacy Boundaries](/proof-requests/privacy-boundaries) for full details.

## Choosing not to respond

You are never required to respond to a Proof Request. If you do not want to submit, simply do not open the link or do not complete the submission. The request will expire on its own.
