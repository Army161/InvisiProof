# Frequently Asked Questions

## What does InvisiProof actually assess?

InvisiProof analyzes observable signals in submitted images, text, and URLs. For images, it looks for visual inconsistencies and patterns. For text, it looks for linguistic patterns associated with fraud or deception. For URLs, it analyzes the structure and domain patterns without visiting the URL from your device.

## What is a Proof Request?

A Proof Request is a two-party workflow. You create a request and share a link with someone. They submit evidence privately. You receive only the risk assessment result — not automatically the raw evidence they submitted. This lets you evaluate a claim without requiring the other person to expose more than necessary.

## Does the requester see the evidence I submit?

No. When you respond to a Proof Request, the requester receives the risk assessment result. They do not automatically receive your raw images, text, or files. See [Privacy Boundaries](/proof-requests/privacy-boundaries) for details.

## Are results guaranteed to be accurate?

No. InvisiProof provides risk assessments based on observable signals. Results are not verdicts, legal evidence, or guarantees of safety or harm. A Low risk result does not mean content is safe. A High risk result does not mean content is definitely harmful. Always use results as one input among many.

## Can I use my own AI provider (BYOK)?

Yes, on Pro and Max plans. Go to **Profile → AI Provider** to configure your own API key. See [BYOK](/privacy/byok) for details.

## Can I delete my data?

Yes. You can delete individual submissions from your scan history at any time. You can also delete your entire account from **Profile → Delete Account**. See [Delete Account](/account/delete-account) for details.

## How do subscriptions work?

Subscriptions are billed monthly or annually through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time. See [Manage Subscription](/account/manage-subscription) for details.

## Can I get a refund?

Refund requests are handled by Apple (iOS) or Google (Android). InvisiProof cannot issue refunds directly. See [Subscription and Refunds](/legal/subscription-refunds) for details.

## How do I contact support?

Email support@invisiproof.com. We aim to respond within 2 business days.

## Is InvisiProof available on Android?

InvisiProof is available on iOS. Android availability will be announced when available.

## Does InvisiProof visit URLs I submit?

No. When you submit a URL for analysis, InvisiProof analyzes the URL structure and available signals without navigating to the URL from your device. See [Analyze a URL](/assessments/analyze-url) for details.
