# Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is InvisiProof?">
    InvisiProof is a privacy-first risk assessment tool that uses AI to evaluate suspicious images, text messages, and URLs. It helps you identify risk signals in content before you act on it — for example, before clicking a link, sending money, or trusting a document.

    InvisiProof also provides Proof Requests: a two-party workflow where you can ask someone to submit evidence privately and receive only the risk assessment result, not the raw evidence.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How accurate is InvisiProof?">
    InvisiProof analyzes observable signals in submitted content. It is not infallible:

    - Sophisticated deception may produce low risk scores
    - Legitimate content with unusual patterns may produce higher risk scores
    - The AI model can make mistakes

    Risk levels are assessments, not verdicts. A Low Risk result means the observable signals don't indicate elevated risk — not that the content is definitely safe. A Critical Risk result means strong risk signals are present — not that the content is definitely harmful.

    Always use InvisiProof results as one input among many, especially for high-stakes decisions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does InvisiProof store my data?">
    Yes. When you submit content for analysis, it is stored in your private account in Supabase with row-level security. Only you can access it through the API.

    How long it's stored depends on your plan:
    - Free: 7 days
    - Plus: 90 days
    - Pro/Max: Unlimited (until you delete it)

    You can delete any scan at any time. See [Evidence Storage](/privacy/evidence-storage) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does BYOK mean?">
    BYOK stands for Bring Your Own Key. It means you can supply your own API key from an AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google Gemini) so that your assessments are processed through your own provider account rather than InvisiProof's shared infrastructure.

    Your key is stored encrypted in Supabase and is never logged or sent to InvisiProof's application servers. See [BYOK](/privacy/byok) for full details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do Proof Requests work?">
    A Proof Request lets you ask someone to submit evidence privately. You create a request, share a 6-digit code with the other person, and they submit their evidence (image, text, or URL). You receive only the AI-generated risk assessment result — never the raw evidence.

    This privacy boundary is enforced at the database level using Supabase Row Level Security policies. See [Proof Requests Overview](/proof-requests/overview) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are InvisiProof results admissible in court?">
    No. InvisiProof results are not legal evidence and are not admissible in court without independent verification by a qualified professional (such as a digital forensics expert).

    InvisiProof is not a legal service. Results are risk assessments based on observable signals — not expert opinions, not forensic analyses, and not legal conclusions. See the [AI Disclaimer](/legal/ai-disclaimer) for full details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my data if I cancel my subscription?">
    If you cancel your subscription and revert to the Free plan:
    - Scans older than 7 days will be deleted within 24 hours of the downgrade
    - New scans will follow the Free plan's 7-day retention policy
    - Your account remains active

    If you delete your account, all data is permanently deleted regardless of plan. See [Delete Account](/account/delete-account) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is InvisiProof available on iOS?">
    InvisiProof launched on Android and web. iOS availability has not been announced. Follow invisiproof.com for updates.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a web app?">
    Yes. InvisiProof is available as a web app at invisiproof.com in addition to the Android app. The web app has the same features as the Android app.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is InvisiProof priced?">
    InvisiProof offers four plans:
    - **Free**: 2 assessments/month, 7-day history
    - **Plus**: $14.99/month or $99.99/year — 25 assessments/month, 90-day history, BYOK
    - **Pro**: $34.99/month or $249.99/year — 150 assessments/month, unlimited history, priority analysis
    - **Max**: $79.99/month or $599.99/year — unlimited assessments, unlimited history, all features

    See [Plans and Pricing](/account/plans) for full details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is InvisiProof's refund policy?">
    Subscriptions are processed through Google Play. Refunds are handled by Google Play according to their refund policy. InvisiProof does not issue refunds directly for used assessments.

    For billing errors (charged incorrectly, duplicate charges), contact support@invisiproof.com. See [Subscription Refunds](/legal/subscription-refunds) for full details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does InvisiProof use my content to train AI models?">
    No. Your submitted content (images, text, URLs) is not used to train AI models. It is analyzed by the AI model to produce your assessment result and then stored privately in your account. It is not shared with AI providers for training purposes.

    See [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy) for full details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I report a security vulnerability?">
    Email security@invisiproof.com. Do not open a public GitHub issue for security vulnerabilities. See [Security Reporting](/privacy/security-reporting) for full details including response timelines and scope.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
