# Troubleshooting

## Assessment is taking a long time

Assessments typically complete within 30–60 seconds. If an assessment is still pending after several minutes:

1. Check your internet connection
2. Pull down to refresh the results screen
3. If the issue persists, try submitting the content again

## "You have reached your monthly limit"

You have used all your assessments or Proof Requests for the current billing period. Options:

- Wait for your limit to reset at the start of your next billing period
- Upgrade your plan from **Profile → Subscription**

## Image upload fails

- Check that the image is in a supported format (JPEG, PNG, HEIC, WebP)
- Check your internet connection
- Try a smaller image if the file is very large
- Make sure InvisiProof has permission to access your photo library (Settings → InvisiProof → Photos)

## Proof Request link is not working

- The request may have expired — check the status in your Requests tab
- The request may have been cancelled
- Make sure you are sharing the correct link

## Sign-in issues

- Check that you are using the correct email address
- If you forgot your password, use [Reset Password](/account/reset-password)
- If you signed up with Google or Apple, use the same sign-in method

## Subscription not showing after reinstall

1. Go to **Profile → Subscription**
2. Tap **Restore Purchases**

This restores active subscriptions from your App Store or Google Play account.

## Assessment result seems incorrect

InvisiProof provides risk assessments based on observable signals. Results are not guaranteed to be accurate. If you believe a result is significantly wrong, you can:

- Submit the content again for a fresh assessment
- Contact support@invisiproof.com with details

## App crashes or freezes

1. Force-close the app and reopen it
2. Check for app updates in the App Store or Google Play
3. If the issue persists, contact support@invisiproof.com with a description of what happened

## Still having trouble?

Contact support at support@invisiproof.com. Include your account email, a description of the issue, and any relevant screenshots.
