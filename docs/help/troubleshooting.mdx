# Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="App won't load / blank screen">
    **Symptoms:** The app opens to a blank white or black screen, or shows a loading spinner indefinitely.

    **Solutions:**

    1. **Force close and reopen** — swipe the app away from your recent apps and reopen it
    2. **Check your internet connection** — InvisiProof requires an internet connection to load. Try opening a website in your browser to confirm connectivity
    3. **Clear app cache** — on Android, go to Settings → Apps → InvisiProof → Storage → Clear Cache, then reopen the app
    4. **Update the app** — open Google Play and check for an InvisiProof update
    5. **Reinstall the app** — uninstall and reinstall InvisiProof from Google Play. Your account data is stored in the cloud and will be restored when you sign in

    If the issue persists after reinstalling, contact support@invisiproof.com with your device model and Android version.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Assessment stuck in 'analyzing' state">
    **Symptoms:** You submitted content for analysis and the status has been "Analyzing" for more than 5 minutes.

    **Solutions:**

    1. **Pull to refresh** — on the scan history or result screen, pull down to refresh the status
    2. **Check your internet connection** — analysis requires a stable connection
    3. **Wait a bit longer** — during high-load periods, analysis may take up to 2–3 minutes
    4. **Close and reopen the app** — the result may have completed but the UI hasn't updated
    5. **Try again** — if the scan is still stuck after 10 minutes, go back to the scan and tap **Retry Analysis** (if available)

    If a scan is stuck in Analyzing for more than 30 minutes, it has likely failed silently. Contact support@invisiproof.com with the approximate time you submitted the scan and your account email.

    <Note>
    Stuck analyses do not count against your monthly quota. If you are charged for a stuck analysis, contact support.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Proof Request code not working">
    **Symptoms:** You entered a 6-digit code but see "Code not found", "Code expired", or "Invalid code".

    **Solutions:**

    - **"Code not found"** — double-check the code. Codes are case-insensitive but must be exactly 6 digits. Ask the requester to confirm the code
    - **"Code expired"** — the request has passed its expiry time. Ask the requester to create a new Proof Request with a new code
    - **"Already submitted"** — evidence has already been submitted for this code. Each code can only be used once. If you submitted by mistake, contact the requester
    - **"Invalid code"** — the code format is wrong. Proof Request codes are 6 digits (numbers only). Make sure you're not including spaces or letters

    If none of these apply and the code is definitely correct and not expired, contact support@invisiproof.com.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can't sign in / forgot password">
    **Symptoms:** You can't sign in to your account.

    **Solutions:**

    - **Forgot password** — tap **Forgot Password** on the sign-in screen and follow the reset flow. See [Reset Password](/account/reset-password)
    - **Wrong email** — try other email addresses you may have used to sign up
    - **Signed up with Google** — if you originally signed up with Google, use **Continue with Google** instead of email/password
    - **Email not verified** — if you never verified your email, tap **Resend Verification Email** on the sign-in screen
    - **Account doesn't exist** — if you can't find your account with any email, you may not have completed sign-up. Try creating a new account

    If you believe your account exists but you can't access it, contact support@invisiproof.com with the email address you used to sign up.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Purchase not restoring">
    **Symptoms:** You reinstalled InvisiProof or signed in on a new device and your subscription is showing as Free.

    **Solutions:**

    1. **Tap Restore Purchases** — go to Profile → Restore Purchases
    2. **Check Google Play account** — ensure you are signed in to Google Play with the same account you used to purchase the subscription
    3. **Check InvisiProof account** — ensure you are signed in to InvisiProof with the same account you used when you purchased
    4. **Wait and retry** — Google Play subscription verification can take a few minutes. Wait 5 minutes and tap Restore Purchases again
    5. **Check subscription status in Google Play** — open Google Play → Profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions. If InvisiProof is listed as active, the subscription exists but hasn't synced yet

    If your subscription shows as active in Google Play but not in InvisiProof after 30 minutes, contact support@invisiproof.com with your Google Play order number.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Image upload failing">
    **Symptoms:** You try to upload an image for analysis but see an error or the upload never completes.

    **Solutions:**

    1. **Check file size** — very large images (over 20MB) may fail to upload. Try compressing the image or taking a screenshot instead of uploading the original
    2. **Check file format** — InvisiProof supports JPEG, PNG, HEIC, and WebP. Other formats may not be supported
    3. **Check internet connection** — image uploads require a stable connection. Try on Wi-Fi if you're on mobile data
    4. **Try a different image** — if only one specific image fails, the file may be corrupted
    5. **Restart the app** — force close and reopen InvisiProof, then try again

    If uploads consistently fail, contact support@invisiproof.com with your device model and the approximate file size of the image you're trying to upload.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Analysis returning unexpected results">
    **Symptoms:** The risk assessment result seems wrong — a clearly suspicious message is rated Low Risk, or a legitimate document is rated High Risk.

    **Understanding the result:**

    InvisiProof analyzes observable signals only. It does not have context about your relationship with the sender, the history of your communication, or information you know independently. This means:

    - A sophisticated phishing message with no obvious signals may score Low Risk
    - A legitimate urgent message from your bank may score Medium Risk due to urgency language
    - A legitimate invoice with unusual formatting may score High Risk due to layout anomalies

    **What to do:**

    - Read the warning indicators carefully — they explain what specific signals were detected
    - Apply your own judgment alongside the result
    - For high-stakes decisions, seek professional advice regardless of the risk score
    - If you believe the result is consistently wrong for a type of content, contact support@invisiproof.com with examples (without sensitive personal information)

    See [What InvisiProof Can and Cannot Determine](/what-invisiproof-can-determine) for a full explanation of limitations.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Still need help?

If none of the above solutions resolve your issue, contact support@invisiproof.com. Include:
- Your device model and Android version (or browser and OS for web)
- Your app version (Profile → About)
- A description of the issue and steps to reproduce it
- Screenshots if applicable (do not include sensitive personal information)

Response time: 1–2 business days.
