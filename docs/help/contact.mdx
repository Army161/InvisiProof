# Contact

## Support

For help with your account, assessments, Proof Requests, or billing:

**Email**: support@invisiproof.com

We aim to respond within 2 business days. Priority support is available on Plus, Pro, and Max plans.

When contacting support, include:
- Your account email address
- A description of the issue
- Any relevant screenshots or error messages
- Your device type and app version (found in **Profile → About**)

## Security issues

To report a security vulnerability:

**Email**: security@invisiproof.com

See [Security Reporting](/privacy/security-reporting) for our responsible disclosure process.

## Legal and privacy

For privacy requests, data deletion inquiries, or legal matters:

**Email**: privacy@invisiproof.com

## Documentation feedback

If you find an error or have a suggestion for these docs, email support@invisiproof.com with the subject line "Docs feedback".

## Response times

| Plan | Expected response time |
|------|------------------------|
| Free | 5 business days |
| Plus | 2 business days |
| Pro | 2 business days |
| Max | 1 business day |

Response times are targets, not guarantees.
