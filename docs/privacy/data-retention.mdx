# Data Retention

InvisiProof retains different types of data for different periods depending on your plan and the data type.

## Retention by data type

### Scans and assessments

| Plan | Retention period |
|------|-----------------|
| Free | 7 days from scan creation date |
| Plus | 90 days from scan creation date |
| Pro | Unlimited (until you delete or cancel) |
| Max | Unlimited (until you delete or cancel) |

After the retention period, the scan record, evidence (image/text/URL), and assessment result are permanently deleted. Deletion is automatic and cannot be undone.

<Note>
Retention is measured from the scan creation date, not the last access date. Upgrading your plan does not retroactively extend retention for scans that have already been deleted.
</Note>

### Proof Requests

Proof Requests follow the same retention policy as scans, based on the **requester's** plan:

| Requester's plan | Retention period |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| Free | 7 days from request creation date |
| Plus | 90 days from request creation date |
| Pro | Unlimited |
| Max | Unlimited |

When a Proof Request is deleted (automatically or manually), both the request record and the respondent's evidence are deleted.

### Account data

| Data type | Retention |
|-----------|-----------|
| Account (email, password hash) | Until account deletion |
| Profile information | Until account deletion |
| Subscription status | Until account deletion |
| BYOK API keys (encrypted) | Until removed by user or account deletion |

### Analytics data

PostHog analytics events are retained according to PostHog's data retention policy (typically 1 year for event data). Analytics data is anonymized and does not contain scan content.

### Support communications

Support emails sent to support@invisiproof.com are retained for 2 years for quality and compliance purposes.

## What happens when you downgrade your plan

If you downgrade from Pro/Max to Plus or Free, your existing scan history is not immediately deleted. However:
- Scans older than the new plan's retention period will be deleted at the next automatic cleanup cycle (typically within 24 hours of downgrade)
- New scans will follow the new plan's retention policy

**Example:** You downgrade from Pro (unlimited) to Plus (90 days). Scans from 6 months ago will be deleted within 24 hours. Scans from 60 days ago will be retained for another 30 days.

## What happens when your subscription expires

If your subscription expires and you return to the Free plan:
- Scans older than 7 days will be deleted at the next cleanup cycle
- You will lose access to your history beyond 7 days

To avoid losing history, renew your subscription before it expires.

## How to request deletion

### Delete specific scans

See [Evidence Storage](/privacy/evidence-storage) for instructions on deleting individual scans.

### Delete all your data

See [Delete Account](/account/delete-account) for instructions on deleting your account and all associated data.

### Request deletion of Proof Request evidence (as respondent)

If you submitted evidence as a respondent and want it deleted, contact support@invisiproof.com with:
- The approximate date you submitted the evidence
- The challenge text you were shown (if you remember it)
- Your email address (if you have an account)

We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

### GDPR / CCPA data requests

For formal data subject access requests or deletion requests under GDPR, CCPA, or other privacy regulations, contact support@invisiproof.com with the subject line "Data Subject Request". We will respond within 30 days.

## Automatic cleanup schedule

Automatic cleanup runs daily. Scans past their retention period are deleted in batches. There may be a delay of up to 24 hours between when a scan passes its retention date and when it is actually deleted.
