# Data Retention

## Assessment history retention

How long your assessment history is kept depends on your plan:

| Plan | History Retention |
|------|-------------------|
| Free | 7 days |
| Plus | 90 days |
| Pro | Unlimited |
| Max | Unlimited |

After the retention period, assessments older than the limit are automatically removed from your history.

## Account data

Your account information (email, profile, subscription status) is retained as long as your account is active.

## Submitted evidence

Submitted content (images, text, URLs) is retained in your account storage until you delete it or your account is deleted. Retention is not automatically limited by your plan — you control deletion.

## Proof Request submissions

Evidence submitted by respondents through Proof Requests is retained until:

- The respondent deletes their submission
- The associated account is deleted
- InvisiProof's standard data lifecycle processes remove it

## Deleting your data

You can delete individual submissions from your scan history at any time.

To delete your entire account and all associated data, go to **Profile → Delete Account**. See [Delete Account](/account/delete-account) for the full process.

## Backups and residual data

Deleted data may persist in encrypted backups for a limited period before being permanently removed. InvisiProof does not use deleted content for any purpose during this period.

## Legal holds

In limited circumstances, InvisiProof may be required to retain data beyond normal retention periods to comply with legal obligations. This is described in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy).
