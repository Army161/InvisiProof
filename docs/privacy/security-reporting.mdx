# Security Reporting

If you discover a security vulnerability in InvisiProof, please report it responsibly.

## How to report

Email: **security@invisiproof.com**

Include:
- A description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce it
- The potential impact
- Any supporting evidence (screenshots, logs, proof-of-concept)

## What to expect

- We will acknowledge your report within 3 business days
- We will investigate and keep you informed of our progress
- We will notify you when the issue is resolved

## Responsible disclosure

Please do not publicly disclose the vulnerability until we have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address it. We ask for a minimum of 90 days before public disclosure.

## Scope

Reports are welcome for:
- Authentication and authorization issues
- Data exposure or access control bypasses
- Injection vulnerabilities
- Privacy boundary violations in Proof Requests
- Any issue that could expose user data

## Out of scope

- Denial of service attacks
- Social engineering of InvisiProof staff
- Physical security issues
- Issues in third-party services not under InvisiProof's control

## Recognition

We appreciate responsible security research. Researchers who report valid vulnerabilities may be acknowledged in our changelog at their request.
