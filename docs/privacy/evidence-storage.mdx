# Evidence Storage

All content you submit to InvisiProof — images, text, URLs, and Proof Request submissions — is stored privately with scoped access controls.

## How storage works

- Each piece of submitted content is stored with a reference to your user account
- Access is scoped so that only your account can retrieve your submissions
- For Proof Requests, respondent submissions are stored separately from requester accounts — requesters cannot read the raw evidence

## Encryption

Content is stored using encrypted cloud storage. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.

## Who can access your evidence

- **You** — through your authenticated account
- **InvisiProof's server infrastructure** — for processing assessments
- **No other users** — other InvisiProof users cannot access your submissions

InvisiProof staff do not routinely access submitted content. Access may occur in limited circumstances for security investigation, legal compliance, or abuse prevention, as described in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy).

## Deleting evidence

You can delete any submission from your scan history at any time. Deletion removes the content from active storage. See [Data Retention](/privacy/data-retention) for details on backup and residual data.

## Proof Request evidence

Evidence submitted by respondents through Proof Requests is stored with additional access restrictions:

- The requester cannot read the raw evidence
- The respondent can delete their submission at any time
- After deletion, the raw evidence is removed from active storage
