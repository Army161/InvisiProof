# Result Disclosure

InvisiProof is designed around a result-only disclosure model: you receive the risk assessment, not automatically the raw evidence.

## Direct assessments

For content you submit yourself, you receive the full assessment result:

- Risk level and score
- Summary of observable signals
- Warning indicators
- Recommended actions
- Limitations

The raw content (your image, text, or URL) remains in your private storage. You can view it in your history, but it is not shared with anyone else.

## Proof Requests

For Proof Requests, the result-only model is the core privacy protection:

- **Requesters** receive the risk assessment result
- **Requesters do not automatically receive** the raw evidence submitted by the respondent
- **Respondents** control what they submit and can delete it at any time

This separation means a respondent can provide evidence for assessment without handing over their private documents, messages, or images to the requester.

## Sharing results

You can choose to share an assessment result with others (for example, by taking a screenshot or copying the result text). InvisiProof does not prevent you from sharing results you have received.

## What InvisiProof does not share

- Your submitted content with other users
- Your assessment results with third parties (except as required by law or described in the Privacy Policy)
- Your identity with respondents in a Proof Request (unless you include it in the purpose or instructions)
