# Data Flow

This page explains how data moves through InvisiProof from submission to result.

## Direct assessment flow

1. **You submit content** — image, text, or URL — through the app
2. **Content is stored** — privately in your account, scoped to your user ID
3. **Analysis is requested** — you tap "Request Analysis Now"
4. **AI model processes the content** — observable signals are extracted
5. **Result is normalized** — into a standard risk assessment format
6. **Result is stored** — in your account history
7. **You see the result** — risk level, score, summary, indicators, actions

## Proof Request flow

1. **You create a request** — purpose and instructions are stored
2. **Respondent opens the link** — they see the purpose and consent notice
3. **Respondent submits evidence** — stored privately, scoped to the request
4. **AI model processes the evidence** — same analysis pipeline as direct assessments
5. **Result is stored** — linked to your request, not to the raw evidence
6. **You see the result** — risk assessment only; raw evidence is not surfaced to you

## What leaves your device

- Submitted content (image, text, URL) is transmitted to InvisiProof's servers over an encrypted connection
- Account credentials are transmitted securely for authentication
- App usage analytics are collected (see [Analytics](/privacy/analytics) for what is and is not collected)

## What stays on your device

- Your session token (stored securely in device storage)
- Cached assessment results for offline viewing

## Third-party services

InvisiProof uses the following categories of third-party services:

- **Cloud storage**: for private evidence storage
- **AI providers**: for assessment processing (default: InvisiProof's server infrastructure; optional: your own API key via BYOK)
- **Authentication**: for account management
- **Analytics**: for app usage metrics (content is never included)

Specific third-party providers are listed in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy).
