# Manage Subscription

InvisiProof subscriptions on Android are managed through Google Play. This page covers subscribing, cancelling, restoring purchases, and billing.

## How to subscribe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the upgrade screen">
    In InvisiProof, tap **Profile** → **Upgrade Plan** (or tap the upgrade prompt when you hit your assessment limit).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Review the plan options (Plus, Pro, Max) and select the one that fits your needs. You can choose monthly or annual billing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tap Subscribe">
    Tap **Subscribe** on your chosen plan. Google Play's purchase flow opens.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm purchase">
    Confirm the purchase in Google Play. Your Google account's payment method is charged.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Access your plan">
    After purchase confirmation, your plan upgrades immediately. Your new assessment quota and history retention take effect right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to cancel

Subscriptions are cancelled through Google Play, not through InvisiProof directly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Google Play">
    Open the Google Play Store app on your Android device.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go to subscriptions">
    Tap your profile icon → **Payments and subscriptions** → **Subscriptions**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find InvisiProof">
    Find InvisiProof in your subscriptions list and tap it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Cancel subscription">
    Tap **Cancel subscription** and follow the prompts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After cancellation:
- Your subscription remains active until the end of the current billing period
- You will not be charged again
- At the end of the billing period, your account reverts to the Free plan
- Scans older than 7 days will be deleted within 24 hours of the downgrade

<Note>
Cancelling through InvisiProof's app settings is not supported — you must cancel through Google Play. This is a Google Play requirement for all Android subscriptions.
</Note>

## How to restore purchases

If you reinstall InvisiProof or sign in on a new device and your subscription is not showing:

1. Tap **Profile** → **Restore Purchases**
2. InvisiProof will check your Google Play account for active subscriptions
3. If a valid subscription is found, your plan will be restored

If restore doesn't work:
- Ensure you are signed in to InvisiProof with the same account you used to purchase
- Ensure you are signed in to Google Play with the account that holds the subscription
- Wait a few minutes and try again — there can be a delay in Google Play's subscription verification
- Contact support@invisiproof.com if the issue persists

## Billing cycle

- **Monthly plans**: Billed on the same day each month as your original purchase date
- **Annual plans**: Billed once per year on the anniversary of your original purchase date
- **Renewal**: Google Play automatically renews your subscription unless you cancel before the renewal date

## What happens when a subscription expires

If your subscription expires (cancelled and billing period ended, or payment failed):
- Your account reverts to the Free plan (2 assessments/month, 7-day history)
- Scans older than 7 days are deleted within 24 hours
- You can resubscribe at any time to restore your plan

## Payment issues

If your payment fails:
- Google Play will retry the payment according to its retry policy
- You will receive a notification from Google Play about the failed payment
- Your subscription remains active during the retry period
- If payment is not resolved, your subscription will be cancelled by Google Play

For payment issues, contact Google Play support — InvisiProof does not handle payment processing directly.
