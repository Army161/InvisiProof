# Manage Subscription

## View your current plan

Go to **Profile → Subscription** to see your current plan, usage, and billing details.

## Upgrade your plan

1. Go to **Profile → Subscription**
2. Tap the plan you want to upgrade to
3. Confirm the purchase

Your new plan takes effect immediately. Usage limits are updated right away.

## Downgrade your plan

1. Go to **Profile → Subscription**
2. Tap the plan you want to downgrade to
3. Confirm the change

Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period. You keep your current plan's features until then.

## Cancel your subscription

To cancel, manage your subscription through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android):

**iOS**: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → InvisiProof → Cancel Subscription

**Android**: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → InvisiProof → Cancel

After cancellation, your plan remains active until the end of the current billing period. After that, your account reverts to the Free plan.

## Restore a purchase

If you reinstall the app or sign in on a new device and your subscription is not showing:

1. Go to **Profile → Subscription**
2. Tap **Restore Purchases**

This restores any active subscriptions associated with your App Store or Google Play account.

## Refunds

Refund requests are handled by Apple (iOS) or Google (Android). InvisiProof cannot issue refunds directly. See [Subscription and Refunds](/legal/subscription-refunds) for details.
