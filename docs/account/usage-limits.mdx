# Usage Limits

Each InvisiProof plan includes a monthly assessment quota. This page explains what counts toward the quota, when it resets, and what happens when you reach the limit.

## What counts as an assessment

Each of the following counts as **one assessment** against your monthly quota:

| Action | Counts as |
|--------|-----------|
| Analyze an image | 1 assessment |
| Analyze text | 1 assessment |
| Analyze a URL | 1 assessment |
| Proof Request (respondent submits evidence) | 1 assessment against the **requester's** quota |

**What does NOT count:**
- Viewing a completed assessment result (no charge)
- Viewing your scan history (no charge)
- Creating a Proof Request (no charge — only the analysis when evidence is submitted counts)
- Failed analyses (if analysis fails due to a server error, it does not count against your quota)

## Monthly quota by plan

| Plan | Assessments per month |
|------|----------------------|
| Free | 2 |
| Plus | 25 |
| Pro | 150 |
| Max | Unlimited |

## When the quota resets

Your quota resets on the same day each month as your subscription start date.

**Example:** If you subscribed on March 15, your quota resets on April 15, May 15, and so on.

For Free plan users, the quota resets on the same day each month as your account creation date.

<Note>
The reset is based on calendar date, not a rolling 30-day window. If you use all 25 Plus assessments on the 1st of the month, you wait until your reset date — not 30 days later.
</Note>

## How to check remaining assessments

Your remaining assessments are shown in:
- **Profile** → **Usage** — shows current usage and quota
- The **Scan** tab — a usage indicator shows how many assessments you have left this month
- The assessment quota banner (shown when you are close to or at the limit)

## What happens when you hit the limit

When you reach your monthly quota:

1. A **quota banner** appears at the top of the Scan tab
2. Tapping **Analyze Image**, **Analyze Text**, or **Analyze URL** shows an upgrade prompt
3. You cannot run new assessments until your quota resets or you upgrade

You can still:
- View your existing scan history
- View completed assessment results
- Respond to Proof Requests (as a respondent — this counts against the requester's quota, not yours)
- Create Proof Requests (the assessment runs when the respondent submits, which counts against your quota at that time)

## Upgrading when at the limit

If you hit your limit and need to run more assessments immediately:

1. Tap the upgrade prompt or go to **Profile** → **Upgrade Plan**
2. Select a higher plan
3. After purchase confirmation, your new quota takes effect immediately
4. You can run assessments right away

## Unused assessments

Unused assessments do not roll over to the next month. If you use 10 of your 25 Plus assessments in a month, the remaining 15 do not carry forward.

## Assessment failures

If an assessment fails due to a server error (not a user error), it does not count against your quota. You will see an error message and can retry the assessment. If you are charged for a failed assessment, contact support@invisiproof.com.
