# Usage Limits

## What counts toward your limit

- **Assessments**: each time you request analysis of an image, text, or URL
- **Proof Requests**: each time you create a new Proof Request

Viewing existing results, browsing history, and managing your account do not count toward limits.

## Monthly limits by plan

| Plan | Assessments/month | Proof Requests/month |
|------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Free | 2 | 1 |
| Plus | 25 | 8 |
| Pro | 150 | 50 |
| Max | Fair-use unlimited | 250 |

## When limits reset

Usage limits reset at the start of each billing period. For monthly plans, this is the same day each month as your subscription start date. For annual plans, limits reset monthly on the same day.

## What happens when you reach your limit

When you reach your monthly assessment or Proof Request limit, you will see a message explaining that you have used your allocation for the month. You can:

- Wait for the limit to reset at the start of your next billing period
- Upgrade to a higher plan for more capacity

## Fair-use unlimited (Max plan)

The Max plan includes fair-use unlimited assessments. This means there is no hard monthly cap, but usage that significantly exceeds normal patterns may be reviewed. InvisiProof reserves the right to contact Max plan users whose usage appears automated or abusive.

## Checking your usage

Go to **Profile → Subscription** to see how many assessments and Proof Requests you have used this month and how many remain.
