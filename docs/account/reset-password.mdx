# Reset Password

If you've forgotten your password or want to change it, you can reset it through the app or web.

## Reset your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the sign-in screen">
    Open InvisiProof and tap **Sign In**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tap Forgot Password">
    Below the password field, tap **Forgot Password**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    Enter the email address associated with your InvisiProof account and tap **Send Reset Link**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check your email">
    Open the password reset email from InvisiProof. It contains a link to reset your password.

    <Note>
    The reset link expires after 1 hour. If it expires, repeat the process to get a new link.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tap the reset link">
    Tap the link in the email. On Android, this opens InvisiProof directly via a deep link. On web, it opens the reset password page in your browser.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your new password">
    Enter your new password (minimum 8 characters) and confirm it. Tap **Reset Password**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in with your new password">
    You are redirected to the sign-in screen. Sign in with your new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If the email doesn't arrive

1. **Check your spam folder** — password reset emails sometimes end up in spam
2. **Wait a few minutes** — email delivery can be delayed
3. **Check the email address** — make sure you entered the correct address associated with your account
4. **Try again** — go back to Forgot Password and request a new link
5. **Contact support** — if you still don't receive the email after 15 minutes, contact support@invisiproof.com

## Change your password (when signed in)

If you are already signed in and want to change your password:

1. Tap **Profile** → **Security Settings**
2. Tap **Change Password**
3. Enter your current password and your new password
4. Tap **Save**

## Google Sign-In users

If you signed up with Google, you don't have an InvisiProof password. Your authentication is handled by Google. To change your Google account password, visit myaccount.google.com.

## Password requirements

- Minimum 8 characters
- No maximum length
- All characters are accepted (letters, numbers, symbols)
- We recommend using a password manager to generate a strong, unique password

## Security note

InvisiProof will never ask for your password via email, chat, or phone. If you receive a message asking for your password, it is not from InvisiProof — report it to security@invisiproof.com.
