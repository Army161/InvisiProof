# Create an Account

Creating an InvisiProof account gives you access to scan history, Proof Requests, and subscription features.

## Sign up with email and password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open InvisiProof">
    Open the InvisiProof app on Android or visit the web app at invisiproof.com.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tap Sign Up">
    On the welcome screen, tap **Sign Up** (or **Create Account**).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your email and password">
    Enter your email address and choose a strong password. Your password must be at least 8 characters.

    <Note>
    Use an email address you have access to — you will need to verify it before your account is fully active.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Agree to terms">
    Review and accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tap Create Account">
    Tap **Create Account**. A verification email will be sent to your address.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Open the verification email and tap the verification link. This opens InvisiProof and confirms your email address.

    If you don't see the email within a few minutes, check your spam folder. You can also tap **Resend Verification Email** in the app.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start using InvisiProof">
    After verification, you are signed in and ready to run your first assessment. You start on the Free plan with 2 assessments per month.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sign up with Google

1. On the welcome screen, tap **Continue with Google**
2. Select your Google account
3. Grant the requested permissions
4. You are signed in immediately — no email verification required

## What happens after account creation

After creating your account:
- You are on the **Free plan** (2 assessments/month, 7-day history)
- Your scan history is empty
- BYOK is not configured (available on Plus and above)
- You can immediately run assessments and create Proof Requests

## Guest mode

You can use some InvisiProof features without an account in guest mode:
- Respond to Proof Requests (enter a code and submit evidence)
- View a single assessment result (not saved to history)

Guest mode limitations:
- No scan history
- No Proof Request creation
- No subscription features
- Evidence submitted as a guest is not linked to an account and cannot be retrieved later

To convert a guest session to a full account, tap **Sign Up** at any time.

## Account security

- Passwords are hashed using bcrypt — InvisiProof never stores your plain-text password
- Email verification prevents account creation with addresses you don't own
- You can enable additional security measures in Profile → Security Settings
