# Create an Account

## Sign up with email

1. Open InvisiProof
2. Tap **Sign Up**
3. Enter your email address and choose a password
4. Tap **Create Account**
5. Check your email for a verification link
6. Tap the link to verify your email address

After verification, you are signed in and ready to use InvisiProof.

## Sign up with Google or Apple

1. Open InvisiProof
2. Tap **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple**
3. Follow the prompts to authorize InvisiProof
4. Your account is created automatically

No email verification is required when signing up with Google or Apple.

## Free plan

All new accounts start on the **Free plan**:

- 2 assessments per month
- 1 Proof Request per month
- 7-day history

You can upgrade to a paid plan at any time from **Profile → Subscription**.

## Account requirements

- A valid email address (for email sign-up)
- You must be 13 years of age or older to use InvisiProof
- You must agree to the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy)
