# Sign-In Options

InvisiProof supports three ways to sign in.

## Email and password

Sign in with the email address and password you used when creating your account.

1. Open InvisiProof
2. Tap **Sign In**
3. Enter your email and password
4. Tap **Sign In**

If you forgot your password, tap **Forgot Password** to reset it. See [Reset Password](/account/reset-password).

## Google

Sign in with your Google account.

1. Open InvisiProof
2. Tap **Continue with Google**
3. Select your Google account and authorize InvisiProof

## Apple

Sign in with your Apple ID.

1. Open InvisiProof
2. Tap **Continue with Apple**
3. Authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password

Apple Sign In may use a private relay email address. This is normal and does not affect your account.

## Switching sign-in methods

If you created your account with email and want to use Google or Apple (or vice versa), contact support at support@invisiproof.com. We can help link your accounts.

## Security

InvisiProof uses secure authentication. Passwords are never stored in plain text. Session tokens are stored securely on your device.
