# Sign-In Options

InvisiProof supports two sign-in methods on Android and web.

## Email and password

Sign in with the email address and password you used when creating your account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open InvisiProof">
    Open the app or visit the web app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Sign In">
    Tap **Sign In** on the welcome screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Enter your email address and password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Sign In">
    Tap **Sign In**. If your credentials are correct, you are signed in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Google Sign-In

Sign in with your Google account for a faster, password-free experience.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Continue with Google">
    On the sign-in screen, tap **Continue with Google**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your account">
    Choose the Google account associated with your InvisiProof account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant permissions">
    Grant the requested permissions (email address and basic profile).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Apple Sign-In is not available on Android. If you previously signed in with Apple on another platform, use email/password or Google to sign in on Android.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting sign-in issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Forgot your password">
    Tap **Forgot Password** on the sign-in screen. Enter your email address and tap **Send Reset Link**. Check your email for a password reset link.

    See [Reset Password](/account/reset-password) for full instructions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Email not recognized">
    If you see "No account found with this email address":
    - Check for typos in your email address
    - Try the email address you used when signing up (you may have used a different one)
    - If you signed up with Google, use **Continue with Google** instead of email/password
    - If you still can't find your account, contact support@invisiproof.com
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Incorrect password">
    If you see "Incorrect password":
    - Check that Caps Lock is not on
    - Try the **Forgot Password** flow to reset your password
    - If you signed up with Google, you may not have a password — use **Continue with Google**
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Email not verified">
    If you see "Please verify your email address":
    - Check your inbox for the verification email
    - Check your spam folder
    - Tap **Resend Verification Email** on the sign-in screen
    - If the email still doesn't arrive, contact support@invisiproof.com
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Google Sign-In fails">
    If Google Sign-In fails:
    - Ensure you have a Google account configured on your device
    - Check that you have an internet connection
    - Try signing out of Google on your device and signing back in
    - If the issue persists, try email/password sign-in instead
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Account locked or suspended">
    If your account has been suspended for a policy violation, you will see a message explaining the reason. Contact support@invisiproof.com to appeal.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Staying signed in

InvisiProof keeps you signed in using a secure session token stored on your device. You will remain signed in until you:
- Tap **Sign Out** in Profile settings
- Delete the app
- Your session expires (after extended inactivity)

## Signing out

To sign out: tap **Profile** → **Sign Out** → confirm.

Signing out does not delete your data. Your scan history and account remain intact.
