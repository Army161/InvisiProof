# Delete Account

You can delete your InvisiProof account at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

## How to delete your account

1. Go to **Profile**
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete Account**
3. Read the confirmation message
4. Tap **Confirm Delete**

Your account is deleted immediately.

## What gets deleted

When you delete your account:

- Your profile and account information
- All assessment history and results
- All submitted content (images, text, URLs)
- All Proof Requests you created
- Your subscription record (does not cancel App Store/Google Play billing — see below)

## What is not automatically deleted

- **App Store / Google Play subscription**: Deleting your InvisiProof account does not cancel your subscription billing. You must cancel separately through the App Store or Google Play. See [Manage Subscription](/account/manage-subscription).
- **Proof Request submissions by respondents**: Evidence submitted by respondents through your Proof Requests is stored separately. Respondents can delete their own submissions independently.

## Backup and residual data

Deleted data may persist in encrypted backups for a limited period before being permanently removed. This is standard practice for cloud services and does not affect your ability to use InvisiProof — your account is inaccessible immediately after deletion.

## Before you delete

- Download or note any assessment results you want to keep — they cannot be recovered after deletion
- Cancel your subscription through the App Store or Google Play to stop future billing
