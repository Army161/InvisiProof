# Delete Account

You can permanently delete your InvisiProof account at any time. Deletion removes all your data from InvisiProof's systems.

<Warning>
Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be deleted and cannot be recovered. Read this page carefully before proceeding.
</Warning>

## How to delete your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Profile settings">
    Tap the **Profile** tab at the bottom of the screen.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Scroll to the bottom">
    Scroll to the bottom of the Profile screen to find the **Delete Account** option.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tap Delete Account">
    Tap **Delete Account**. A confirmation screen appears explaining what will be deleted.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm deletion">
    Read the confirmation carefully. Type your email address or password to confirm you understand the action is permanent.

    Tap **Permanently Delete My Account**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Account deleted">
    Your account and all associated data are deleted. You are signed out of the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What gets deleted

When you delete your account, the following is permanently deleted:

| Data | Deleted |
|------|---------|
| Your account (email, password hash) | Yes |
| All scans (images, text, URLs) | Yes |
| All assessment results | Yes |
| All Proof Requests you created | Yes |
| Evidence submitted in your Proof Requests | Yes |
| Your BYOK API keys (encrypted) | Yes |
| Your subscription record | Yes |
| Your analytics user ID | Yes |

## What cannot be recovered

After deletion, none of the above data can be recovered. InvisiProof does not maintain backups of deleted user data that can be restored on request.

If you think you might want your data in the future, consider:
- Exporting your scan history before deleting (Profile → Export Data, if available)
- Taking screenshots of important assessment results
- Downloading evidence images before deleting

## Active subscriptions

If you have an active subscription when you delete your account:

1. **Cancel your subscription first** through Google Play (see [Manage Subscription](/account/manage-subscription))
2. Then delete your account

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your Google Play subscription. If you delete your account without cancelling, you may continue to be charged by Google Play. Cancel through Google Play before deleting your account.

## Grace period

There is no grace period for account deletion — deletion is immediate and permanent once confirmed.

If you deleted your account by mistake, contact support@invisiproof.com immediately. We may be able to assist if you contact us within 24 hours of deletion, but we cannot guarantee recovery.

## Deleting data without deleting your account

If you want to delete your scan history without deleting your account:

1. Go to **Profile** → **Data and Privacy**
2. Tap **Delete All Scans**
3. Confirm deletion

This deletes all your scans and assessment results but keeps your account active.

## Data deletion requests (GDPR/CCPA)

For formal data deletion requests under GDPR, CCPA, or other privacy regulations, contact support@invisiproof.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will process your request within 30 days.
