# Plans and Pricing

InvisiProof offers four plans to fit different usage levels and privacy needs.

## Plan comparison

| Feature | Free | Plus | Pro | Max |
|---------|------|------|-----|-----|
| **Assessments per month** | 2 | 25 | 150 | Unlimited |
| **Scan history** | 7 days | 90 days | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **BYOK support** | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Proof Requests** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Priority analysis** | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| **All future features** | No | No | No | Yes |
| **Monthly price** | Free | $14.99/mo | $34.99/mo | $79.99/mo |
| **Annual price** | Free | $99.99/yr | $249.99/yr | $599.99/yr |

Annual plans save approximately 44% compared to monthly billing.

## Free

The Free plan is designed for occasional use and evaluation.

- **2 assessments per month** — enough to evaluate the product and handle occasional suspicious content
- **7-day history** — recent scans are accessible for one week
- **Proof Requests** — create and respond to Proof Requests (counts toward your assessment quota)
- **No BYOK** — assessments run through InvisiProof's shared AI infrastructure

## Plus — $14.99/month or $99.99/year

Plus is designed for regular personal use.

- **25 assessments per month** — enough for daily use and occasional high-volume periods
- **90-day history** — three months of scan history for reference and review
- **BYOK support** — use your own OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google Gemini API key
- **All Free features**

## Pro — $34.99/month or $249.99/year

Pro is designed for power users and professionals who rely on InvisiProof regularly.

- **150 assessments per month** — high-volume usage for professionals
- **Unlimited history** — all scans retained indefinitely until you delete them
- **Priority analysis** — your assessments are processed ahead of Free and Plus users during high-load periods
- **BYOK support**
- **All Plus features**

## Max — $79.99/month or $599.99/year

Max is designed for organizations and heavy users who need no limits.

- **Unlimited assessments** — no monthly cap
- **Unlimited history**
- **Priority analysis**
- **All future features** — Max subscribers get access to new features as they launch
- **BYOK support**
- **All Pro features**

## Frequently asked questions about plans

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What counts as an assessment?">
    Each time you run an analysis (image, text, or URL) counts as one assessment. Proof Request submissions also count as one assessment against the requester's quota. See [Usage Limits](/account/usage-limits) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When does my monthly quota reset?">
    Your quota resets on the same day each month as your subscription start date. For example, if you subscribed on the 15th, your quota resets on the 15th of each month.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I upgrade or downgrade?">
    Yes. You can change your plan at any time through Google Play (Android) or your account settings (web). Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my history if I downgrade?">
    If you downgrade to a plan with a shorter history retention, scans older than the new plan's retention period will be deleted within 24 hours of the downgrade taking effect. See [Data Retention](/privacy/data-retention) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    InvisiProof does not currently offer a free trial for paid plans. The Free plan lets you evaluate the product with 2 assessments per month.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How to subscribe

Subscriptions are managed through Google Play on Android. See [Manage Subscription](/account/manage-subscription) for instructions.
