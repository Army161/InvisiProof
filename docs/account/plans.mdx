# Plans

InvisiProof offers four plans. All plans include the core assessment features.

## Free

**$0/month**

- 2 assessments per month
- 1 Proof Request per month
- 7-day history
- Standard AI provider

## Plus

**$14.99/month** or **$99.99/year** (save $79.89/year)

- 25 assessments per month
- 8 Proof Requests per month
- 90-day history
- Standard AI provider
- Priority support

## Pro

**$34.99/month** or **$249.99/year** (save $169.89/year)

- 150 assessments per month
- 50 Proof Requests per month
- Unlimited history
- Standard AI provider
- BYOK support
- Priority support

## Max

**$79.99/month** or **$599.99/year** (save $359.89/year)

- Fair-use unlimited assessments
- 250 Proof Requests per month
- Unlimited history
- Standard AI provider
- BYOK support
- Priority support

## Plan comparison

| Feature | Free | Plus | Pro | Max |
|---------|------|------|-----|-----|
| Assessments/month | 2 | 25 | 150 | Fair-use unlimited |
| Proof Requests/month | 1 | 8 | 50 | 250 |
| History | 7 days | 90 days | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| BYOK | — | — | Yes | Yes |
| Priority support | — | Yes | Yes | Yes |

## Upgrading or downgrading

You can change your plan at any time from **Profile → Subscription**. See [Manage Subscription](/account/manage-subscription) for details.

## Annual billing

Annual plans are billed once per year. The savings shown are compared to paying monthly for 12 months.
